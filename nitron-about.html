About Nitron

Staff you
switch on.

Nitron is an AI bot agency. We build named, hand-made AI bots that do real jobs — admin, creative, sales, marketing, trading — then hand them over ready to work inside the tools you already use.

The idea

Bots that work like staff.

Most AI tools wait for you to prompt them. Nitron bots don't. Each unit has a name, a job description, and a lane. They keep your calendar honest, keep your feed fed, answer your customers, and watch your numbers — without being reminded to.

Every bot is assembled by hand for the way you actually work, plugged into the chat platform your team already lives in — Discord, Slack, WhatsApp — and handed over working. No dashboards to learn. You just talk to them.

How we build

The maker

Built by one pair of hands.

Nitron is run by Dominic Garcia, a creative designer and bot builder based in Caloocan City, Philippines. Before Nitron, he spent years doing the jobs these bots now do — design, campaigns, client follow-ups, ops — and got tired of watching small teams drown in them.

So every Nitron unit is built the way a designer builds: opinionated, named, and finished. One person builds your bot end to end, and the same person answers when you need it tuned.
